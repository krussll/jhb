Indoor carpet runs fast and true with zero wind or rain. Narrow-to-mid bias bowls allow you to stay tighter to the jack and hold tighter delivery lines on laser-straight carpet rinks without crossing into adjacent rinks.

Can beginners play at {{ club_name }}?

Yes! Most lawn bowls clubs welcome beginners and newcomers. Clubs regularly run introductory roll-ups, taster sessions, and open days where experienced members and coaches guide you through the basics of delivery, line, and weight.

Do I need my own bowls to play at {{ club_name }}?

In most cases, no. Clubs maintain sets of club loan bowls across various sizes (sizes 0 to 4) that new visitors can use during roll-ups or coaching sessions. We recommend trying several sizes at the club before buying your own set.

What shoes and dress code are required at {{ club_name }}?

The most essential requirement is completely flat-soled shoes (trainers with smooth soles, or specialized bowls shoes) to avoid damaging the playing surface.
