            <div class="dp-ref-item">

          <h4 class="h5 font-weight-bold text-dark mb-2">Setup &amp; Execution Steps</h4>
          <ol class="dp-drill-step-list">
            <li><strong>Rink Setup:</strong> Place your delivery mat as you normally would. On the forehand side, place two markers 3 yards in front of the mat with roughly one mat's length between them to form a gate. Repeat on the backhand side.</li>
            <li><strong>Choose Your Gate:</strong> When stepping onto the mat, commit to which gate you are playing through (forehand or backhand).</li>
            <li><strong>Deliver Through the Gate:</strong> Roll the bowl at a comfortable, natural weight directly through the gate. This is pure line control—do not worry about distance.</li>
            <li><strong>Balanced Ends:</strong> Deliver 4 bowls per end: 2 on the forehand gate and 2 on the backhand gate.</li>
            <li><strong>Scoring:</strong> Every bowl that passes cleanly between the markers scores 1 point (maximum 4 points per end).</li>
            <li><strong>Complete 10 Ends:</strong> Tally your final score out of 40 and compare against the benchmarks below.</li>
          </ol>

          <h4 class="h5 font-weight-bold text-dark mb-2">Progressive Variations</h4>
          <div class="dp-drill-variations">
            <div class="dp-var-box dp-var-box--easier">
              <strong><i class="fas fa-arrow-down" aria-hidden="true"></i> Easier Variation</strong>
              Move the markers closer to the mat (2 yards), or widen the gate to 2 mat lengths to build early confidence.

              <strong><i class="fas fa-arrow-up" aria-hidden="true"></i> Harder Variation</strong>
