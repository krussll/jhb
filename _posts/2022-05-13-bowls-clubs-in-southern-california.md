Southern California is the undisputed heartland of lawn bowling in the United States. Governed by **Bowls USA's Southwest Division**, the region is home to roughly 25 active clubs stretching from the coastal pines of Cambria through Santa Barbara, Los Angeles, Orange County, the Inland Empire, and down to San Diego Bay.

Whether you want to roll beside the Pacific Ocean at **Laguna Beach Lawn Bowling Club** in Heisler Park, step onto historic Hollywood-era greens in **Beverly Hills** and **Santa Monica**, compete in U.S. Open tournaments at **Long Beach** and **Newport Harbor**, or enjoy fast year-round synthetic greens in **Coronado** and **Sun City**, Southern California offers year-round sunshine and some of North America's finest bowling surfaces.

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear Rules &amp; Sun Preparation</h3>

<p>Before stepping onto a Southern California bowling green, keep two practical rules in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat, heel-less footwear is mandatory:</strong> Both delicate Bermuda grass greens and synthetic carpets can be damaged by heels or deep athletic treads. Smooth flat-soled sneakers are fine for your first lesson, while dedicated flat-soled bowling shoes are recommended once you join a club. See our <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Grip &amp; sun comfort:</strong> Warm, dry Southern California afternoons can make bowls feel slicker in the hand. Many local players prefer dimpled or deeply grooved grips (or a light grip enhancer) alongside a wide-brimmed sun hat. Check our <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-grip-enhancers/' | relative_url }}">Best Grip Enhancers Guide</a> for options.</li>
</ol>
