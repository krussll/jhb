South East Queensland is a global powerhouse of lawn bowls. Blessed with year-round subtropical sunshine, Queensland pairs a relaxed outdoor lifestyle with some of the finest championship bowling greens on Earth.

From the sun-drenched riverbank greens of **Merthyr Bowls Club** in Brisbane's New Farm to the world-renowned greens of **Broadbeach Bowls Club**—host of both the 2018 Commonwealth Games and 2023 World Bowls Championships—and the air-conditioned dome arena of **Pine Rivers**, South East Queensland provides a paradise for both competitive pennant bowlers and social barefoot enthusiasts.

<h2>Green Speeds &amp; Best Bowls for Queensland Conditions</h2>

<p>Bowling in Brisbane and on the Gold Coast is defined by warm subtropical sunshine, high humidity, afternoon coastal breezes, and fast, velvety Tifdwarf hybrid couch greens. Greens regularly clock between 15 and 18 seconds, demanding narrower trajectories and touch weight control.</p>

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear Rules &amp; Hand Sizing</h3>

<p>Before rolling your first bowl on a Queensland green, keep these essential considerations in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat-soled footwear is mandatory on greens:</strong> Natural couch greens and woven synthetics are delicate. For social barefoot bowls, players bowl in bare feet, socks, or smooth flat thongs/sneakers. For pennants and club play, approved flat bowling shoes without heels or grooves are required. Consult our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for tournament-compliant options.</li>
  <li><strong>Select the right bowl size to prevent grip strain:</strong> A bowl that is too large or too heavy will slip off your fingers in humid or sweaty conditions. Use our practical <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> to measure your hand before purchasing.</li>
</ol>
