Florida is the third-largest lawn bowling state in the United States and the beating heart of **Bowls USA's Southeast Division (SED)**. Fueled by year-round sunshine and a thriving winter snowbird community arriving from Canada, the northern states, and the UK, the region spans 15 verified clubs across **Central Florida, Tampa Bay, the Gulf Coast, South Florida, North Carolina, and Tennessee**.

Whether you want to step onto historic **St. Petersburg Lawn Bowling Club** (est. 1916, listed on the National Register of Historic Places), bowl under the floodlights on **Mount Dora's** 14 Har-Tru rinks, compete on three immaculate grass greens at **Sun City Center**, travel north to **Pinehurst Resort's** championship ditched greens in North Carolina, or enjoy rooftop social bowls at **Fogg Street Lawn Club** in Nashville, this guide connects you to every active green in the Southeast.

<h3>Practical Tips for Bowling in Florida &amp; the Southeast</h3>

<p>Before stepping onto a Southeast Division green, keep four local realities in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Plan around the November&ndash;April snowbird season:</strong> Florida clubs operate year-round, but winter (November through April) is peak season for daily jitneys, open tournaments, and visiting Canadian and northern US bowlers. In North Carolina (<a href="{{ '/bowls-clubs/florida-southeast-usa/pinehurst-lawn-bowls-club/' | relative_url }}">Pinehurst</a> and <a href="{{ '/bowls-clubs/florida-southeast-usa/asheville-lawn-bowling-club/' | relative_url }}">Asheville</a>), main outdoor play runs spring through autumn.</li>
  <li><strong>Prioritise grip in subtropical humidity:</strong> High Florida humidity and sunscreen can make smooth bowls slippery at release. Choose embedded grips (such as Aero Z-Scoop or Taylor Progrip), carry a dry shammy towel, and keep a quality grip enhancer in your bag—see our <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-grip-enhancers/' | relative_url }}">Best Grip Enhancers Guide</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Rinse Har-Tru dust &amp; wear flat-soled shoes:</strong> Every club enforces smooth, heel-less footwear to protect delicate grass, Har-Tru, and synthetic surfaces. After playing on Har-Tru rinks at Mount Dora or Delray Beach, wipe bowls clean with a damp cloth so fine stone dust does not build up in the grips. Check our <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Verify community gate access before visiting:</strong> Several active Florida clubs—including Sun City Center, Kings Point, Maple Leaf, The Villages, World Parkway, and Pelican Preserve—sit inside 55+ residential communities. Visiting Bowls USA members are often welcomed for open tournaments or guest draws, so contact the club secretary ahead of time to arrange gate entry.</li>
</ol>
