Lawn bowls in Auckland, New Zealand, is famous worldwide for two distinct traditions: premier natural **Cotula weed greens** and a thriving modern social barefoot culture. Surrounded by two magnificent harbours—the Waitemata and the Manukau—Auckland provides a stunning maritime backdrop for the sport of bowls.

From the national championship rinks of **Carlton Cornwall Bowls** in Greenlane and heritage greens of **Remuera** (est. 1894) to buzzing Friday evening barefoot sessions along Jervois Road at **Ponsonby Bowling Club**, Auckland's clubs offer warm Kiwi hospitality, competitive inter-club pennants, and accessible community bowls.

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear Rules &amp; Hand Sizing</h3>

<p>Before rolling your first bowl on an Auckland green, keep these essential considerations in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat-soled footwear is mandatory on greens:</strong> Cotula weed turf and woven synthetics are delicate. For social barefoot bowls, players bowl in bare feet, socks, or smooth flat thongs/sneakers. For pennants and club play, approved flat bowling shoes without heels or grooves are required. Consult our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for tournament-compliant options.</li>
  <li><strong>Select the right bowl size to prevent grip strain:</strong> A bowl that is too large or too heavy will slip off your fingers in dry conditions, causing inconsistent weight control. Use our practical <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> to measure your hand before purchasing.</li>
</ol>
