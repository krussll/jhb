  - question: "What is the difference between embedded grips and non-embedded grips?"
    answer: "Embedded grips (like the Aero Z-Scoop or Drakes Pride Pro-50 channel) are milled deeper into the shoulder of the bowl, allowing your fingertips to sit comfortably inside the body. Non-embedded grips (like standard dimples or Aero Dentations) sit flush on the running edge without changing the bowl's external diameter."
  - question: "Do deep grips make a lawn bowl feel smaller in the hand?"
    answer: "Yes. Because deep channel grips (such as Aero's Z-Scoop) allow the fingertips and thumb to wrap further into the body of the bowl, they can effectively make a size 3 bowl feel like a size 2.5 or 2 in your delivery hand."
  - question: "Which hand delivery grip (Claw vs Cradle) works best with gripped bowls?"

<h2>Before You Change Your Grip: Crucial Checks</h2>

<h3>1. Check Bowl Size</h3>

<p>There is a traditional belief in bowls that "bigger is better" because a heavier bowl maintains momentum and is harder for opponents to dislodge. However, a heavier bowl should never come at the expense of your grip control.</p>

<p>To perform the standard water grip test: submerge your bowl and bowling hand in a bowl of water. Grip the bowl normally and hold it out with your hand on top. If the bowl slips from your grasp under gravity, the bowl is too large or the grip is insufficient for your hand strength.</p>

<h2>Detailed Reviews: The Best Factory Lawn Bowl Grips</h2>

<h3 id="aero-z-scoop">1. Aero Z-Scoop &mdash; <span class="badge badge-success">Deepest Ergonomic Channel</span></h3>

<p>The <strong>Aero Z-Scoop</strong> is arguably the most influential grip innovation in modern bowls. Developed using precision robotic lathe milling, the Z-Scoop cuts parabolic curved recesses directly into the shoulder of the bowl.</p>

<p>By recessing the finger contact points deeper into the wood, the Z-Scoop effectively reduces the grip feel of the bowl by approximately half a size. This allows bowlers with smaller hands or arthritis to comfortably bowl with heavier, full-sized woods without losing hold in cold or wet conditions.</p>

<h3 id="drakes-pride-pro-50-grip">2. Drakes Pride Pro-50 Channel &mdash; <span class="badge badge-primary">Best Recessed Grooving</span></h3>

<p>The <strong>Drakes Pride Pro-50 Channel Grip</strong> was engineered specifically to combat wet British conditions. By cutting deeper, sharper channel grooves into the running shoulder, Drakes Pride created a tactile channel that channels moisture away while giving your fingertips an immovable positive reference point.</p>

<p>It remains the standard choice on Drakes Pride's flagship <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/drakes-pride-pro-50-review/' | relative_url }}">Pro-50 model</a>.</p>

<h3 id="taylor-progrip-xtreme">3. Taylor Progrip & Xtreme Grip &mdash; <span class="badge badge-info">Classic Tactile Feedback</span></h3>

<p>Thomas Taylor has refined its factory grip designs over decades. The <strong>Taylor Progrip</strong> utilizes deep, circular indentations that fit comfortably under standard finger pads. For bowlers wanting wider contact, the <strong>Xtreme Grip</strong> introduces deep oval channels that accommodate multiple finger widths.</p>

<p>Both options deliver flawless release characteristics, ensuring the bowl leaves your hand cleanly without snagging on exit.</p>

<h3 id="henselite-mega-grip">4. Henselite Mega Grip &mdash; <span class="badge badge-secondary">Balanced Multi-Dimple Design</span></h3>

<p>Henselite's <strong>Mega Grip</strong> features an array of precision-milled dimples that provide steady traction without disrupting the clean line of the running edge. It is ideal for players who want a clear fingertip register without the aggressive depth of deep channel grips.</p>

<h3 id="aero-dentated-grip">5. Aero Dentated Grip &mdash; <span class="badge badge-dark">Best Non-Embedded Tactile Option</span></h3>

<p>If you dislike the feeling of recessed channels cutting into your fingers, the <strong>Aero Dentated Grip</strong> is the premier alternative. Milled as crisp zigzag serrations across the running edge, it provides high surface friction while keeping the bowl's exterior profile smooth and continuous.</p>
