<p>Transporting a full set of four bowls, shoes, wet weather gear, and accessories can easily total 8 to 10 kilograms. Hauling that weight by shoulder strap causes unnecessary fatigue before you even deliver your first wood. A dedicated wheeled trolley ensures you arrive at the green relaxed and ready to compete.</p>

<h2>Key Features to Evaluate Before Buying</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Wheel Stability:</strong> Bags with dual wide-track wheels or corner-mounted wheels roll smoothly over grass, gravel, and steps without tipping sideways.</li>
  <li><strong>Internal Partitioning:</strong> Having separate compartments for your bowls, shoes, and clothing keeps wet gear away from clean fabrics and prevents bowls from clattering together.</li>
  <li><strong>Locker Dimensions:</strong> If you plan to store your bag at your club, check locker height and depth before choosing a taller touring model.</li>
  <li><strong>Handle Durability:</strong> Look for multi-stage aluminium telescopic handles that lock firmly in place at comfortable towing heights.</li>
</ul>

<p>The <strong>Drakes Pride Trolley Bag</strong> is a benchmark fixture in clubhouse corridors for good reason. <p>Inside, two massive tiered sections provide ample room for four bowls, a pair of shoes, waterproof trousers, and mid-layers. Two exterior zipped pockets keep chalk, measures, and scorecards immediately accessible without digging through clothing.</p>

<p>The <strong>Aero ComfitPro Ultraglide</strong> solves one of the most frustrating problems in lawn bowls: buying an expensive wheeled bag only to discover it doesn't fit into your clubhouse locker.
