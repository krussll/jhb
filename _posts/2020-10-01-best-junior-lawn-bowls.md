<h2><a href="#what-junior-bowls-are"></a>What Junior Bowls are</h2>

<p>Junior bowls are lawn bowls designed to be comfortable to hold and use for children and young teens.</p>

<p>They act exactly the same as adult bowls, so can be a great way for children to compete with each other and practice.</p>

<p>As they are not compliant with World Bowls rules they can’t be used in tournaments, but they are fantastic for introducing kids to the game.</p>

<h2><a href="#how-big-are-junior-bowls"></a>How big are junior bowls</h2>

<p>Junior bowls will have a diameter of 98.5mm (3 7/8”). This is compared to a size 00 for an adult (the smallest size available) of 116mm (4 9/16”).</p>

<h2><a href="#why-choose-junior-bowls"></a>Why Choose Junior Bowls?</h2>

<p>Junior bowls should be used when a child is unable to hold a size 00 bowl comfortably.</p>

<p>This will aid their enjoyment of the game and prevent technical delivery hitches that arise when a junior player is using a set of bowls too large or heavy for them.</p>

<h2><a href="#junior-bowls-recommendations"></a>Junior Bowls Recommendations</h2>

<p>There are two primary models specifically manufactured in dedicated junior sizes: the <strong>Taylor Junior Ace</strong> and the <strong>Drakes Pride Junior Professional</strong>. Both are scaled-down versions of their respective adult flagship models.</p>

<h3><a href="#our-1-pick---junior-ace"></a>OUR #1 PICK - Taylor Junior Ace</h3>

<p>The Junior Ace looks and feels like a genuine adult bowl. The colour schemes and finish mimic the adult <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/taylor-ace-review/' | relative_url }}">Taylor Ace</a> range, making it ideal for older children and teens who want equipment that looks serious on the green.</p>

<p>Crucially, Taylor also manufactures their adult Ace model in sizes 0000, 000, and 00. This makes transitioning from junior bowls to full World Bowls-approved competition sets seamless as the young bowler's hands grow.</p>

<h3><a href="#junior-professional"></a>Drakes Pride Junior Professional</h3>

<p>The Drakes Pride Junior Professional is great for younger children taking their very first steps in bowls.</p>

<p>Finished in bright primary colours, these bowls are eye-catching, extremely durable, and built to withstand drops on path edges and bank boards. Based on the proven <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/drakes-pride-professional-review/' | relative_url }}">Drakes Pride Professional</a> profile, they roll with a predictable, gentle arc.</p>

<p>Unfortunately the jump from junior to adult sizes isn't helped by Drakes Pride only offering the smallest 00 sizes in their adult range. For this reason we recommend these for the younger ages - such as primary school children.</p>

<p>Both these models have similar draw arcs and come with no grips on the bowls.</p>
