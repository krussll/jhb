<h2>Best Yellow Lawn Bowls Models Compared (2026)</h2>

{% include roundup-comparison-table.html %}

<p>Modern bowls come in so many colours, it can be hard to choose. However, certain colours are always more popular than others. Yellow is certainly up there as one of the most popular choices around.</p>

<p>This is likely those showing their love for their country (very popular with the Aussies!) or their local sports team, as well as players who want maximum visibility in dull evening light.</p>

<p>But what makes and models can you get in yellow? Let's look at what the major brands offer in 2026.</p>

<h2>Taylor</h2>

<p>Taylor offers two yellow options:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Lemon Sherbet</li>
  <li>Yellow/White</li>
</ul>

<p>The two colours are yellow with white speckles, so they do look very similar—however the yellow is a bit lighter on the Yellow/White option.</p>

<p>These colour choices aren't available on every model in the range—let's have a look at which ones are available where.</p>

<h3>Lemon Sherbet</h3>

<figure><img src="{{ '/img/posts/taylor-ace-lemon-sherbert.png' | prepend: site.baseurl | replace: '//', '/' }}" alt="Taylor Ace Lemon Sherbet yellow lawn bowls" width="217" height="206"/></figure>

<p>Lemon Sherbet is available in the <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/taylor-ace-review/' | relative_url }}">Taylor Ace</a> and <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/taylor-blaze-lawn-bowls-review/' | relative_url }}">Taylor Blaze</a> models.</p>

<p>You can order this colour choice using the custom Build a Bowl feature on the <a href="https://www.taylorbowls.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Taylor Bowls website</a>.</p>

<h3>Yellow/White</h3>

<figure><img src="{{ '/img/posts/taylor-international-yellow-white.png' | prepend: site.baseurl | replace: '//', '/' }}" alt="Taylor International Yellow/White lawn bowls" width="217" height="206"/></figure>

<p>The Yellow/White option is available on the <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/taylor-vector-vs-review/' | relative_url }}">Taylor Vector VS</a> and <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/taylor-international-lawn-bowls-review/' | relative_url }}">Taylor International</a> models.</p>

<p>Taylor sometimes holds coloured bowls in stock, however availability varies throughout the season. Going with the custom Build a Bowl feature ensures you get your exact size, grip, and colourway.</p>

<p>Their traditional black-only models, such as the <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/taylor-legacy-sl-review/' | relative_url }}">Legacy SL</a> and <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/taylor-lignoid-lawn-bowls-review/' | relative_url }}">Lignoid</a>, are not offered in yellow.</p>

<h2>Henselite</h2>

<p>Henselite doesn't always keep plain yellow bowls in permanent UK stock, however they regularly release short runs and limited-edition sets (such as Resort and Australian AFL-inspired editions) on models like the Dreamline XG and Tiger range.</p>

<p>If you want a yellow Henselite set, check authorised stockists early in the season or watch second-hand listings on eBay for limited-run colourways.</p>

<h2>Biased Bowls</h2>

<p>Biased Bowls offers British Racing Green with a distinctive yellow streak down the side on models such as the <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/biased-bowls-racing-line-lawn-bowl-review/' | relative_url }}">Racing Line</a> and Torque.</p>

<h2>Other Brands</h2>

<p>Outside of the brands above, classic black or traditional dark-toned models from Drakes Pride rarely feature solid yellow, though limited two-tone speckle runs occasionally appear through specialist UK dealers.</p>
