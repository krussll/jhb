The West Country boasts one of the most distinguished and accomplished lawn bowls traditions in the British Isles. Spanning the vibrant city greens of Bristol and Bath, the sun-kissed coastal rinks of Weston-super-Mare and Clevedon, the pastoral market towns of Somerset, and the rolling downs of Wiltshire, this region is an exceptional hub for flat green, indoor, and short mat bowling.

The area is also home to world-renowned indoor venues such as Clarrie Dunbar, which regularly hosts World Bowls Tour events and national squad training, alongside thriving community clubs offering relaxed Friday night roll-ups and coaching for newcomers.

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear and Grip Sizing</h3>

<p>Before bowling at any Bristol, Bath, Somerset, or Wiltshire club, keep these essential rules in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat-soled footwear is strictly mandatory:</strong> Both outdoor turf and indoor carpets are easily damaged by ridges, heels, or running treads. Smooth flat sneakers (such as Vans or Converse) are ideal for introductory roll-ups before purchasing dedicated flat-soled bowls shoes. Read our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for approved styles.</li>
  <li><strong>Measure your hand before buying bowls:</strong> Using bowls that are too large leads to dropped deliveries and strain. Check our step-by-step <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> to determine your exact hand measurement.</li>
</ol>
