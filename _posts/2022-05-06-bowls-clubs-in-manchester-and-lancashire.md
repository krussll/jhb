Greater Manchester and Lancashire are the undeniable heartland of British crown green bowling. With hundreds of beautifully undulating greens tucked behind historic pubs, located inside civic parks, or proudly tended by centenary community clubs, the sport is deeply embedded in the region's working-class sporting fabric.

However, the region also features unique flat green gems—such as the championship greens of Heaton Park (which staged the 2002 Commonwealth Games lawn bowls competition) and East Lancashire Bowls Club in Blackburn—alongside premier indoor winter carpet stadiums in Bolton, Blackpool, and Preston.

Understanding the difference between **Crown Green**, **Flat Green**, and **Indoor** formats is essential when looking for a local club in the North West.

<h2>Understanding the Three Bowling Formats in Manchester &amp; Lancashire</h2>

<p>Before purchasing bowls or visiting a green in the North West, it is vital to know which code your chosen club plays:</p>

<div class="row mb-4">
  <div class="col-md-4 mb-3">
    <div class="p-3 bg-light border h-100 shadow-sm" style="border-radius: 0; border-left: 4px solid #1F2B5F !important;">
      <h3 class="h6 font-weight-bold text-dark"><i class="fas fa-mountain text-primary mr-2" aria-hidden="true"></i> 1. Crown Green Bowls</h3>
      <p class="small text-secondary mb-2"><strong>Dominant Code in NW England</strong></p>
      <p class="text-secondary small mb-2">
        Played on an undulating, natural turf green with a convex rise (the "crown") up to 10–12 inches higher in the centre.
      </p>
      <ul class="small mb-0 pl-3">
        <li><strong>No Rinks:</strong> Play can be sent in any direction across the entire green.</li>
        <li><strong>Biased Jack:</strong> The jack itself is biased (with two small white dots) and curves when bowled.</li>
        <li><strong>Bowls:</strong> Smaller, denser bowls weighted in pounds and ounces (usually 2lb 4oz to 2lb 10oz with a 2-full bias) rather than size numbers.</li>
        <li><a href="{{ '/post/are-crown-green-bowls-the-same-as-flat-green-bowls/' | relative_url }}"><strong>Read: Crown vs. Flat Green Guide</strong></a></li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="col-md-4 mb-3">
    <div class="p-3 bg-light border h-100 shadow-sm" style="border-radius: 0; border-left: 4px solid #C89B3C !important;">
      <h3 class="h6 font-weight-bold text-dark"><i class="fas fa-leaf text-success mr-2" aria-hidden="true"></i> 2. Flat Green Bowls</h3>
      <p class="small text-secondary mb-2"><strong>Championship &amp; Games Code</strong></p>
      <p class="text-secondary small mb-2">
        Played on perfectly level grass lawns divided into defined rectangular rinks bounded by ditches.
      </p>
      <ul class="small mb-0 pl-3">
        <li><strong>Strict Rinks:</strong> Bowlers must stay within marked rink boundaries.</li>
        <li><strong>Unbiased Jack:</strong> Delivered straight towards a white or yellow non-biased jack.</li>
        <li><strong>Bowls:</strong> Sized 00 to 4 (e.g. Taylor Ace, Drakes Pride Professional).</li>
        <li><strong>Key Venues:</strong> <a href="{{ '/bowls-clubs/manchester-lancashire/heaton-park-bowls-club/' | relative_url }}">Heaton Park Bowls Club</a> (2002 Commonwealth Games greens) &amp; East Lancashire BC.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="col-md-4 mb-3">
    <div class="p-3 bg-light border h-100 shadow-sm" style="border-radius: 0; border-left: 4px solid #28a745 !important;">
      <h3 class="h6 font-weight-bold text-dark"><i class="fas fa-warehouse text-warning mr-2" aria-hidden="true"></i> 3. Indoor Stadiums</h3>
      <p class="small text-secondary mb-2"><strong>Winter Flat Green Play</strong></p>
      <p class="text-secondary small mb-2">
        Indoor carpet stadiums running throughout autumn and winter (September to April).
      </p>
      <ul class="small mb-0 pl-3">
        <li><strong>Fast Carpets:</strong> Speeds reach 15 to 17 seconds on true, laser-level surfaces.</li>
        <li><strong>Tight-Line Bowls:</strong> Narrower models like the Taylor Vector VS are preferred over wide outdoor bowls.</li>
        <li><strong>Key Venues:</strong> <a href="{{ '/bowls-clubs/manchester-lancashire/bolton-indoor-bowling-club/' | relative_url }}">Bolton Indoor Bowls Club</a> (6 rinks) and Blackpool Indoor Bowls Centre.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear and Hand Sizing</h3>

<p>Before bowling at any Manchester or Lancashire green, keep these gear essentials in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Completely flat-soled footwear is required:</strong> On crown greens, walking across steep slopes in shoes with ridges or heels tears the manicured grass. Smooth, flat sneakers (such as skate shoes) or regulation flat bowling shoes are required. Read our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for approved styles.</li>
  <li><strong>Crown Green Bowls Sizing:</strong> In crown green bowling, equipment is classified by weight (e.g. 2lb 4oz, 2lb 6oz, 2lb 8oz) and density (Standard, High Density, Deluxe). Most adult men use 2lb 8oz to 2lb 10oz; most adult women use 2lb 4oz to 2lb 6oz. Check our <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> before buying.</li>
</ol>
