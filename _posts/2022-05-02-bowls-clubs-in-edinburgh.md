Lawn bowls in Edinburgh has a proud heritage dating back centuries. From the historic greens of Dean Bowling Club (established in 1838) and Edinburgh Bowling Club beside the Meadows (instituted in 1848) to vibrant community clubs across Corstorphine, Leith, Portobello, and Morningside, the Scottish capital is one of the most active flat green bowling regions in the United Kingdom.

Whether you are looking to join a competitive Edinburgh Bowling League team, attend a relaxed weekend beginner roll-up, or head indoors during the winter months to keep your touch sharp, Edinburgh and the Lothians boast exceptional facilities for bowlers of all ages and abilities.

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear and Grip Sizing</h3>

<p>Before stepping onto any Edinburgh bowling green, keep these two gear essentials in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat-soled footwear is strictly required:</strong> Both natural grass greens and indoor carpets are easily damaged by ridges, heels, or running treads. For your first roll-up, smooth flat sneakers (such as skate shoes) are acceptable at most clubs. When playing regularly, invest in regulation flat bowling footwear. Read our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for approved options.</li>
  <li><strong>Measure your hand before buying bowls:</strong> Using bowls that are too large leads to finger strain, slipping deliveries, and dropped weight control. Check our step-by-step <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> to determine your exact hand measurement before purchasing.</li>
</ol>
