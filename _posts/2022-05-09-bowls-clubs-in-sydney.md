Lawn bowls in Sydney is defined by breathtaking natural scenery, rich sporting tradition, and an unmatched social barefoot culture. Whether you are rolling alongside the crashing Pacific waves on the clifftops of **Clovelly**, soaking up harbour views at **The Greens North Sydney**, or enjoying live music and local craft ales at the community-owned **Petersham Bowling Club**, Sydney offers a bowling experience unlike anywhere else in the world.

From **Balmain Bowling Club**—established in 1880 as the oldest club in New South Wales—to competitive powerhouses like **St Johns Park** and **Cabramatta** in Western Sydney, the Harbour City caters to every level of bowler.

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear Rules &amp; Hand Sizing</h3>

<p>Before stepping onto a Sydney green, keep these practical tips in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat-soled footwear is mandatory on greens:</strong> Natural couch greens are delicate. For social barefoot bowls, players bowl in bare feet, socks, or smooth flat thongs/sneakers. For pennants and club play, approved flat bowling shoes without heels or grooves are required. Consult our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for tournament-compliant options.</li>
  <li><strong>Select the right bowl size to prevent grip strain:</strong> A bowl that is too large or too heavy will slip off your fingers in dry conditions, causing inconsistent weight control. Use our practical <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> to measure your hand before purchasing.</li>
</ol>
