Adelaide and South Australia have a deep-rooted lawn bowling culture dating back to the late 19th century. From the historic greens of Adelaide Bowling Club (established in 1897 in the East Parklands) and Adelaide Oval Bowling Club (instituted in 1904) to bustling coastal clubs along Gulf St Vincent and revolutionary undercover dome complexes, Adelaide provides one of Australia's most dynamic bowls scenes.

Whether you are looking to contest high-level Saturday pennants in the Metropolitan Bowls Association (MBA), gather friends for a relaxed evening of floodlit summer Night Owls, or organise a corporate barefoot roll-up with food and drinks, Adelaide's clubs welcome bowlers of every generation and skill level.

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear Rules &amp; Hand Sizing</h3>

<p>Before rolling your first bowl on an Adelaide green, keep these essential equipment considerations in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat-soled footwear is mandatory on greens:</strong> Natural couch greens and woven synthetics are delicate. For social barefoot bowls and Night Owls, bare feet, socks, or smooth flat thongs/sneakers are welcomed. For pennants and club play, approved flat bowling shoes without heels or grooves are required. Consult our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for tournament-compliant options.</li>
  <li><strong>Select the right bowl size to prevent grip strain:</strong> A bowl that is too large or too heavy will slip off your fingers in dry conditions, causing inconsistent weight control. Use our practical <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> to measure your hand before purchasing.</li>
</ol>
