<h2>The Top 7 Bowls Brands Profiled</h2>

<h3 id="1-aero">1. Aero Bowls &mdash; <span class="badge badge-success">#1 For Robotic Innovation</span></h3>

<p>Aero Bowls has shaken up the bowling industry since its inception. Utilizing advanced computer-guided lathes and robotic polishing systems, Aero produces bowls with unparalleled manufacturing repeatability—ensuring every set matches down to microscopic tolerances.</p>

<p>Aero is also famous for its patented "Harbour Bridge" trajectory, which creates a smooth, continuous trajectory that avoids severe late-turn hooks. Crucially, Aero is the only brand to offer half sizes (e.g., 2.5, 3.5), making them a dream for players caught between standard sizes.</p>

<h3 id="2-taylor">2. Thomas Taylor &mdash; <span class="badge badge-primary">#1 For Heritage & Flat Finishes</span></h3>

<p>Founded in Glasgow in 1796, Thomas Taylor is the oldest and most respected manufacturer in the sport. Taylor woods are celebrated for their flat, predictable finishing lines, making them an immense favourite across the UK and international greens.</p>

<p>Their benchmark bowl, the <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/taylor-ace-review/' | relative_url }}">Taylor Ace</a>, is arguably the most successful club bowl in history, performing effortlessly across grass speeds from 11 to 16 seconds.</p>

<h3 id="3-henselite">3. Henselite &mdash; <span class="badge badge-info">#1 For Australian Innovation</span></h3>

<p>Founded by the Hensell family in Melbourne in 1930, Henselite forever changed lawn bowls by developing the first composite plastic woods, replacing traditional lignum vitae timber. Today, Henselite remains an international powerhouse, known for its high-performance Tiger family of bowls.</p>

<h3 id="4-drakes-pride">4. Drakes Pride &mdash; <span class="badge badge-secondary">#1 For British Greens</span></h3>

<p>Drakes Pride has manufactured championship bowls in Liverpool since 1982. They were the very first bowls company to harness computerized manufacturing, setting the standard for accuracy on natural outdoor turf.</p>

<p>The <a href="{{ '/review/bowls/drakes-pride-professional-review/' | relative_url }}">Drakes Pride Professional</a> remains one of the UK's best-selling bowls, renowned for its forgiving trajectory on uneven grass heads.</p>
