Melbourne is Australia's sporting capital, and its lawn bowls culture is second to none. From the historic greens of **Melbourne Bowling Club** in Windsor—officially instituted in March 1864 as the oldest lawn bowls club in Australia—to sunny Friday afternoon barefoot sessions in Fitzroy North and St Kilda, lawn bowls is woven into Melbourne's social fabric.

Whether you are targeting high-stakes Bowls Victoria Saturday pennant competition, organising a corporate barefoot celebration with food trucks and local craft beers, or seeking all-weather undercover bowling beneath a massive canopy dome, Melbourne offers a world-class venue for every bowler.

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear Rules &amp; Hand Sizing</h3>

<p>Before rolling your first bowl on a Melbourne green, keep these essential equipment considerations in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat-soled footwear is mandatory on greens:</strong> Natural couch greens and woven synthetics are delicate. For social barefoot bowls, bare feet, socks, or smooth flat thongs/sneakers are welcomed. For pennants and club play, approved flat bowling shoes without heels or grooves are required. Consult our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for tournament-compliant options.</li>
  <li><strong>Select the right bowl size to prevent grip strain:</strong> A bowl that is too large or too heavy will slip off your fingers in dry conditions, causing inconsistent weight control. Use our practical <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> to measure your hand before purchasing.</li>
</ol>
