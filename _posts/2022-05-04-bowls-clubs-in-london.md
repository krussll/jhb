  - question: "What footwear is required when visiting a London bowls club?"
    answer: "To prevent damage to manicured turf and indoor needle-punch carpet, clubs require flat-soled footwear with no heels, ridges, or heavy treads. Clean, flat sneakers (such as Vans or Converse) are perfectly acceptable for initial trial sessions before buying dedicated bowling shoes."
  - question: "What bias lawn bowl is best for London greens?"
    answer: "London park greens typically run between 11 and 13 seconds during dry summer spells. For fast indoor carpets like Crystal Palace (running at 15 to 17 seconds), a slimmer, narrower-drawing bowl like the Taylor Vector VS is recommended."

London possesses one of the most diverse and historic lawn bowls scenes in the world. Tucked behind Victorian mansion blocks, nestled inside celebrated public parks from Battersea to Hampstead Heath, and operating inside state-of-the-art winter indoor arenas, the capital's bowling greens offer a tranquil sporting escape from the bustle of city life.

Whether you are looking to join an established club competing in the Middlesex or Surrey County leagues, organise a fun Friday evening social roll-up with friends and craft beers, or maintain your form through the winter at an indoor stadium, London's bowls clubs provide something for everyone.

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear and Grip Sizing</h3>

<p>Before heading to a London bowling club, remember these two essentials:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat-soled footwear is strictly required:</strong> Both natural turf greens and indoor needle-punch carpets can be easily scuffed by heels or ridged running treads. For your first taster session, smooth flat trainers (such as skate shoes or plimsolls) are fine. Check out our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for regulation footwear.</li>
  <li><strong>Measure your hand before purchasing:</strong> Bowling with a size too large causes hand fatigue and inaccurate delivery. Use our <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> to determine your exact hand measurement.</li>
</ol>
