Lawn bowls in Perth, Western Australia, combines Mediterranean sunshine, dramatic natural waterways, and a fiercely competitive pennant structure. Perched along the blue waters of the Swan and Canning rivers or nestled along the Indian Ocean coastline, Perth boasts some of the most scenic lawn bowls settings on Earth.

From the breathtaking clifftop vistas of **Mosman Park Bowling Club** and the CBD skyline views of **South Perth** (est. 1898) to the buzzing Friday evening barefoot sessions in **Leederville** and **Subiaco**, Perth welcomes casual social bowlers and elite Premier League competitors alike.

<h3>Essential Gear: Footwear Rules &amp; Hand Sizing</h3>

<p>Before rolling your first bowl on a Perth green, keep these essential equipment tips in mind:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Flat-soled footwear is mandatory on greens:</strong> Natural couch greens and woven synthetics are delicate. For social barefoot bowls, bare feet, socks, or smooth flat thongs/sneakers are welcomed. For pennants and club play, approved flat bowling shoes without heels or grooves are required. Consult our <a href="{{ '/help/lawn-bowls-dress-code/' | relative_url }}">Lawn Bowls Dress Code Guide</a> and <a href="{{ '/guide/best-lawn-bowls-shoes/' | relative_url }}">Best Lawn Bowls Shoes Guide</a> for tournament-compliant options.</li>
  <li><strong>Select the right bowl size to prevent grip strain:</strong> In dry, hot summer conditions, bowls can slip if they are oversized. Use our practical <a href="{{ '/help/what-size-lawn-bowl-should-i-use/' | relative_url }}">Bowl Sizing Guide</a> to measure your hand before purchasing.</li>
</ol>
