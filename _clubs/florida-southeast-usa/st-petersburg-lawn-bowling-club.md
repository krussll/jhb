  Founded in 1916 adjacent to the historic Mirror Lake Shuffleboard Complex in downtown St. Petersburg, the St. Petersburg Lawn Bowling Club is the oldest lawn bowling club in Florida and the tenth oldest in the United States. Listed on the National Register of Historic Places, the club maintains fast, finely prepared playing surfaces and a classic clubhouse steeped in Gulf Coast history. Visitors and beginners are warmly welcomed for introductory sessions with complimentary club loan bowls.
