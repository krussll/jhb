  Instituted in 1867, Queen's Park Bowling Club is one of Glasgow's most storied and welcoming community sports institutions. Located beside Queen's Park in Glasgow's vibrant Southside, the club features two 6-rink natural turf greens, a traditional clubhouse with licensed bar and function hall, and competitive men's, women's, and senior teams in the Glasgow Bowling Association leagues. Free coaching and loan sets are provided for prospective members every spring.
