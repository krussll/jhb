  Established in 1897 in Adelaide's scenic East Parklands (Rymill Park), Adelaide Bowling Club is South Australia's inaugural bowls club. Featuring three meticulously curated natural turf greens, the club caters to elite Metropolitan Bowls Association pennant players as well as social roll-ups. Its prime parkland location makes it Adelaide's premier destination for corporate barefoot bowls functions, Friday night social roll-ups, and summer Night Owls competitions, complete with floodlit greens and full clubhouse bar facilities.
