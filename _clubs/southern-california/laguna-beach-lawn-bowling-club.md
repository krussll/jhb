  Perched directly above the Pacific Ocean in Laguna Beach's scenic Heisler Park, the Laguna Beach Lawn Bowling Club is widely regarded as one of the most picturesque bowling venues in North America. Free introductory lessons, loan bowls, and daily social draws make it exceptionally welcoming for beginners and visiting bowlers alike.
