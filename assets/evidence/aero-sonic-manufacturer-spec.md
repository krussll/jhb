# Aero Sonic Manufacturer Specifications

- **Source:** [Aero Bowls UK – Sonic Model Overview](https://www.aerobowls.com/product/sonic) (accessed 2024-02-15)
- **Bias profile:** Listed as the widest bias in the Aero lineup with Harbour Bridge trajectory for a consistent, even finish on outdoor greens.
- **Available sizes:** Supplied in UK sizes 00 to 5 in heavyweight, with optional half sizes via custom orders.
- **Grip options:** Offered with Zig-Zag, Z-Scoop, Dentations, and plain rings as standard choices with no surcharge.
- **Colour program:** Customers can select from Solid, Speckle, Duo, Trifecta, Quad, Picasso, and Rainbow colourways assembled to order.
