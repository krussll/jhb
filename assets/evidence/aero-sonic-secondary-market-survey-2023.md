# Aero Sonic Secondary Market Survey (Q4 2023)

- **Date range checked:** 1 September – 30 November 2023
- **Platforms:** eBay UK, Bowls Classifieds Facebook group, Bowls Trader UK forum
- **Method:** Weekly saved-search alerts for "Aero Sonic" filtered to bowls category, noting listings with price, size, and condition.
- **Findings:**
  - eBay UK produced 2 listings in the period, both new-old-stock priced £335–£360 plus shipping.
  - Bowls Classifieds recorded 1 lightly used set (size 3H) at £320; sold within 24 hours of posting.
  - Bowls Trader UK forum recorded no Aero Sonic listings; ISO posts (n=4) received no responses.
- **Conclusion:** Genuine second-hand stock appeared fewer than one listing per month across monitored channels, supporting the assessment that availability is limited compared with other models routinely showing 6–8 listings per month.
