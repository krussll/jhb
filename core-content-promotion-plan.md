# Core Content Promotion Plan

## Objectives
- Spotlight the twelve core articles and two evergreen hubs (Reviews and Drill Pack) identified as the site’s foundational resources.
- Guide new and returning visitors toward the most valuable pathways from the moment they land on the homepage or other high-traffic entry points.
- Reinforce the four newcomer goals (Learn the essentials, Choose your bowls, Practice smarter, Compete with confidence) across navigation, in-content signposts, and promotional assets.

## 1. Homepage Experience
1. **Hero module refresh**
   - Swap the current minimal hero on `/index.html` for a hero card that promotes the "Getting Started" hub and showcases the four pathways with clear CTAs.
   - Add a secondary CTA that jumps straight to the "Learn the essentials" pathway for first-time visitors.
2. **Featured pathways section**
   - Introduce a section directly below the hero that surfaces the four core goals with iconography and brief benefit statements.
   - For each pathway, display the top article plus a link to the relevant hub (e.g., Reviews or Drill Pack).
3. **Persistent promo strip**
   - Add a slim banner (visible on desktop and mobile) that rotates highlights for the drill pack and bowl-buying guides.
   - Schedule seasonal swaps (e.g., preseason tactics) to keep the strip fresh without losing core focus.

## 2. Navigation & Site Architecture
1. **Primary navigation updates**
   - Add a top-level "Start Here" link leading to `/getting-started/`.
   - Group equipment content under a "Choose Your Bowls" mega-menu panel with quick links to the sizing guide and buying guide.
2. **Secondary navigation / footer**
   - Add a "Core Guides" column in the footer that lists the four goal-based pathways.
   - Include the drill pack download as a persistent footer CTA.
3. **Breadcrumb adjustments**
   - Ensure posts within the four goal categories display breadcrumbs that map back to the relevant pathway hub.

## 3. Getting Started Hub Enhancements
1. **Modular hero**
   - Introduce tabbed navigation for the four goals so users can preview the key articles without scrolling.
2. **Action cards**
   - Build reusable cards for each core article that include estimated reading time, skill level, and the next recommended step.
3. **Conversion blocks**
   - Add an email capture block offering the drill pack in exchange for subscribing.
   - Feature social proof (testimonials or reader quotes) near the tactics pathway to build credibility.

## 4. In-Article Promotion
1. **Contextual sidebars**
   - On long-form guides, include a sticky sidebar widget linking to the related pathway pillar content.
2. **End-of-post CTAs**
   - Standardize a closing block that routes readers to the next article in the pathway.
3. **Inline callouts**
   - Use stylized callout boxes to promote the drill pack within practice-focused articles and the buying guide within gear content.

## 5. Content Discovery & SEO
1. **Structured data**
   - Add Article structured data to each core post to improve search visibility and link them to the corresponding pathway via `about` or `knowsAbout` properties.
2. **Internal linking audit**
   - Update top-performing blog posts to link into the core twelve articles, prioritizing contextual anchor text.
3. **Topic clusters**
   - Create category landing pages (e.g., `/learn-the-essentials/`) that aggregate the relevant posts with descriptive intro copy.

## 6. Promotion & Retention
1. **Email onboarding series**
   - Develop a four-email welcome flow, each dedicated to one pathway with featured articles and quick wins.
2. **Social media snippets**
   - Schedule recurring social posts that highlight key takeaways from each core article and link back to the site.
3. **On-site messaging**
   - Use exit-intent or time-on-page triggers to offer the drill pack download before visitors bounce from practice-related pages.

## 7. Measurement & Iteration
1. **Analytics dashboards**
   - Track click-through rates from the homepage hero, pathways section, and footer links to gauge engagement.
2. **Heatmaps & session recordings**
   - Monitor interaction with the new hero and tabbed modules to validate layout effectiveness.
3. **Quarterly review**
   - Reassess which core posts drive the most conversions and adjust the homepage and navigation placements accordingly.

## Implementation Phases
- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2):** Homepage hero and pathways section, nav updates, footer column, standardized end-of-post CTAs.
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4):** Getting Started hub enhancements, modular cards, sticky sidebar widget, inline callouts.
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 5-6):** Structured data rollout, topic clusters, analytics dashboards, email onboarding workflow.
- **Ongoing:** Seasonal promo updates, internal linking audits, performance reviews.
