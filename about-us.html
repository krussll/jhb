Independent reviews, bias analysis, and coaching drills refined over 15 years on the green.

Hi, I’m Reece Williams, founder of Jack High Bowls. I created this resource to solve a problem every bowler encounters: finding unbiased, practical advice on equipment without the confusing jargon or sales spin.

Choosing the right set of bowls can be daunting—a set is a significant investment, and manufacturer bias charts rarely reflect how bowls actually behave across fast indoor carpet or heavy outdoor turf. My goal is to provide fluff-free, evidence-based guides that help you pick the right model with confidence.

Our Mission

Jack High Bowls exists to demystify bowls equipment through transparent, on-rink testing and practical coaching guides, empowering bowlers of every level to invest wisely and enjoy the game for decades to come.

Editorial Standards & Independence

To maintain trust with readers and search engines alike, our testing and reporting adhere to strict principles:

  • Hands-On Testing: Every review is tested on regulation rinks across multiple ends, measuring true running lines, draw arcs, and grip stability. Learn more in our comprehensive Review Methodology.
  • Commercial Independence: We do not accept payment, free gear, or sponsorship in exchange for favorable reviews. All opinions are entirely our own.
  • Ethics & Disclosure: Recommendations follow the Chartered Institute of Marketing (CIM) code of ethics. For affiliate details, read our affiliate policy and privacy commitments.
  • Fact-Checking & Revisions: We revisit our gear guides at least twice per season to account for manufacturer stamp changes and weight/bias adjustments.
