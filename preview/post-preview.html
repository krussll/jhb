Taylor Legacy SL vs Henselite Tiger TX: which is best?

We put two heavyweight draw shot bowls head-to-head so you can pick the line and feel that suits your game.

Last updated 20 March 2023 · 7 min read

If you are weighing up two modern heavyweights, you need a clear look at how each model behaves end after end. We matched the Taylor Legacy SL against the Henselite Tiger TX to see which bowl gives you the confidence to attack the head in changing conditions.

Bias and line shape

The Legacy SL keeps a steady, medium bias that feels predictable on club greens. The Tiger TX holds its line longer before sweeping late, which is brilliant for drawing around traffic but needs a confident release.

Weight control

Tiger TX’s profile makes it a touch more forgiving on heavy surfaces. The Legacy SL rewards a smooth finish with minimal wobble, making it a favourite for leads chasing consistency.
